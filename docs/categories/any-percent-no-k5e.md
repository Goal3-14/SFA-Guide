# Any% No K5E

Any% No K5E (also **NK5** or **BMC**) is an unrestricted full-game category allowing all glitches **except** [K5E](../skips/k5e.md). Allows multiple files in order to warp position between files using [ESWs](../glitches/esw-checkpoints.md), escaping softlocks and enabling more major skips.

All files used during the run must be **made during the run** &mdash; pre-made files are not allowed (that's New Game+).

This category skips: K2, CC2, LFV, WC1 (SS3), VFP2. **Optimal route ~3h30m.**

## Route options

### Shield Hover vs DIME

For the first major trick in the run there is the option of doing either [Shield Hover](../skips/shield-hover.md) or [Dark Ice Mines Early (DIME)](../skips/dime.md). Shield Hover is the most difficult trick in the game to do consistently and is only recommended if you are actively trying to get WR &mdash; highly recommended you avoid it and do DIME instead, which is similar to K4E (which is required anyway).

### DIM completion

For completing Dark Ice Mines there are two possible routes:

- **Seam Walk &mdash;** enter the lower mines mostly as intended, then do [Seam Walk](../skips/seam-walk.md) to skip to the final boss. More beginner-friendly, safer, easier to learn. **Slower by ~5:30.**
- **Bike Skip &mdash;** do [Bike Skip / Dismount Glitch](../skips/bike-skip.md) to skip straight from unlocking the mammoth to the final boss room. Multiple parts including navigating both Fox and the bike separately through the bike tunnels while the camera constantly moves and flashes &mdash; very difficult to look at. Failing the trick at the end loses **7 minutes**, making this the much riskier option. Not recommended for first learning, but possible.

These tricks have no effect outside of DIM &mdash; the rest of the route is identical regardless of choice.

## Game versions

All glitches and tricks for this category can be used on **any version** of the game. Using JP text saves 4&ndash;5 seconds. v1.1 saves 15 seconds over v1.0. See [Versions](../general-info/versions.md).
