# 100%

100% requires you to collect and submit **all Spellstones and Krazoa Spirits**, as well as obtaining all magic upgrades, shop upgrades, maps, and depositing all cheat tokens in the maze. **Multiple files allowed.**

Optimal route is around **5 hours**.
