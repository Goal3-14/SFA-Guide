# Versions

The game has 3 regions (PAL, US, JP) and 2 versions (v1.0 and v1.1).

- All JP discs are v1.0.
- All PAL discs are v1.1.
- US discs can be either v1.0 or v1.1.

## Identifying region

The regions can be easily worked out by the front of the disc:

- **JP** has a unique white design.
- **PAL** and **US** share a blue and orange design. PAL says "PAL" on the disc; US doesn't.

All discs say "EUR" (PAL), "USA", or "JPN" at the bottom to identify their region. US/JP discs cannot be played on PAL consoles and vice versa, even if the console region is changed.

## Identifying US version

For US discs, the version can be determined by text on the inner ring on the back of the disc. The back says `DOL-GSAE-0-`, followed by 2 digits:

- `00` = v1.0
- `01` = v1.1

Be aware that on the back of US v1.0 discs they may say JP instead of US &mdash; v1.0 US and JP discs are identical, so this is not an issue.

## v1.0

v1.0 has a unique trick: in this version Fox has collision with explosive barrels, allowing for [Damage Clip](../skips/damage-clip.md), which clips you out of bounds in Ice Mountain. **This trick is a requirement for the [Any%](../categories/any-percent.md) category, meaning Any% can only be run on v1.0.**

While performing certain tricks such as [Gold Bar Skip](../skips/gbs.md), the game will load areas without textures, displaying default textures instead. On v1.0 these are coloured a mix of red, green, blue, and yellow.

## v1.1

In Dragon Rock at the end of the Hightop section the ending cutscene will trigger **12 seconds faster** than on v1.0. Parts of the game also load slightly faster &mdash; overall v1.1 saves **15 seconds** over v1.0 on full game runs.

When the default-texture state triggers, the textures on v1.1 are coloured grey instead of multi-colour.

## PAL

PAL has the same 15 seconds of time save over v1.0 as PAL is v1.1. However PAL runs at 50fps instead of 60fps, which leads to small changes due to things missed when converting to 50fps. There is no major gameplay, routing or trick difference between PAL and US v1.1 except one: due to the framerate difference [Shield Hover](../skips/shield-hover.md) may be much more difficult to perform on PAL. (Shield Hover is already an extremely difficult trick only recommended if going for WR.)

## Language

For full game categories playing with **JP text saves 4&ndash;5 seconds** over English text. PAL cannot use JP text. For US and JP discs, the language is automatically set to whatever region the console is set to &mdash; the console is allowed to be modded to change the region to JP.
