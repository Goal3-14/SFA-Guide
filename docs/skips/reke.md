# REKE (Red-Eye King Early)

**Location:** Walled City 1 &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Waterfall Jump](../glitches/waterfall-jump.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [Waterfall Jump](../glitches/waterfall-jump.md)

## What it skips

During WC1 you're normally required to collect Super Ground Quake, Sun Tooth, and Moon Tooth. REKE allows you to **skip straight to the Red-Eye King boss fight** to collect SS3.

## Setup

1. Do [Waterfall Jump](../glitches/waterfall-jump.md) to get OOB and gain [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md).
2. Swim under or around the map to the centre.
3. Cancel air swim on the ramp downwards.
4. Navigate the edge of the room while ensuring you hit triggers to get to the boss fight trigger.

## Two ways to reach the centre

### Safe (slower)

Swim across the left side of the map to the next corner, then easily swim around the water to get to the centre while staying OOB. **Essentially risk-free.**

### Waterfall Clip (faster but harder)

1. Go to the same waterfall as [Waterfall Jump](../glitches/waterfall-jump.md) to **gain swim height**.
2. Cancel your swim on the edge of the water just barely still OOB.
3. With a precise position and angle, fall off the map and land in the water with enough downwards momentum to keep you further under the map than usual.
4. Cross underneath the water without going back in bounds.

**The harder but much faster strat.**
