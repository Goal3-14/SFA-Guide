# CC3 Airswim

**Location:** Cape Claw 3 &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md)

## When it's needed

CC3 is the version of Cape Claw intended to be done to enter OFP2 after Dragon Rock. During CC2 you're intended to collect 2 fire gems and lower the water to open the path to OFP. If CC2 was skipped, the path will be closed in CC3 and the water still raised.

CC3 Airswim lets you reach OFP anyway.

## Setup

In CC3, once the cannon is used to open the entrance to inside the head, you can interact with the still-raised water. **Entering and exiting the water gives you an airswim** which can be used to swim around the outside of the map to reach OFP.

1. **Target and slowly sidestep** into the water. Leave the water immediately as you start swimming &mdash; otherwise you get stuck inside the head.
2. Swim to the **back right** of the map.
3. Swim around the **outside of the right side** of the map (the side where you enter Cape Claw).
4. When turning right at the corner near the head, **stay close to the edge of the wall** to ensure you don't softlock.
5. Once you hit the loading zone for the block puzzle room joining CC to OFP, **zoom the camera** to get the map to load.
6. Enter the block puzzle room. You'll have the swim height to reach the top layer of the room and complete it as normal.

## Leaving OFP2

When leaving OFP2 you'll need to do [OFP Cam Lock](ofp-cam-lock.md) to leave &mdash; the fire gem gate in CC is still closed and blocking your exit.
