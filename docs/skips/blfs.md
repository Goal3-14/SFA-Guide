# BLFS (Baby Lightfoot Skip)

**Location:** Lightfoot Village &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md)

Allows collecting the fuel cells in the Lightfoot hut that would normally require completing LFV and finding baby lightfoots in order to access the hut. **Only used for the All Overworld Fuel Cells (AOFC) category.**

## Video

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ygD7TP3pg8>

(The main video does a slightly worse swim when entering LFV; the better setup is documented further down.)

## Approaching LFV

After doing the [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md) movement, when dropping into the water **go right instead of left**, then perform a memory leak (see the full example video). Remember to avoid targeting while reloading the map. Then swim towards LFV.

When approaching, swim towards the deloaded block (marked in the LFV reference image) and stop when reaching the corner of it. Turn so you are facing the corner of the back block, then you can swim straight forward while hitting and dodging all necessary triggers. Swim forward until you are close to the basement, then **turn your camera left**.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LMQJ8BKGcjI>

## Whirlpool danger zones

Typically in the water around LFV there are whirlpools which affect your swimming. They only load if you are close enough **and** looking in their direction. While in the deloaded state, trying to load these whirlpools will **crash the game**.

In the LFV reference image there are coloured regions:

- **Right green region:** you must be facing **left** at all times to avoid loading the whirlpool and crashing.
- **Left orange region:** you must be facing **right** at all times.
- **Two purple areas:** safe zones where you turn around during the trick.

## Continuing the route

After swimming forward and getting close to the basement you'll be in the bottom purple region and turn to face left. Keep holding target to keep your facing angle.

Once facing left, swim backwards to hit the higher water level to gain height and break your airswim. Leave LFV through the main gate then re-enter &mdash; when leaving, make sure you go far enough as there's a trigger you need to hit to load the fuel cells.

Follow the centre building wall to reach the top purple area, then safely turn around and swim across the water to rocket boost to the higher area.

## Final stretch

Once reaching the spot shown in the final reference image, you will be about to enter the **orange region** &mdash; make sure you are facing right (opposite to how Fox is facing in the image) and hold target. Back-walk until the bottom layer of the fuel cell house is to your right, then release target and jump down. You are **still in the orange region** so be careful not to turn the camera around fully.

Climb the ladder and collect the cells without turning the camera. Save reload to fix the area and continue.
