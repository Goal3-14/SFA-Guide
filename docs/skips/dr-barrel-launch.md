# DR Barrel Launch

**Location:** Dragon Rock &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Saves ~7 seconds**

## How it works

When a barrel carrier tries to pick up a barrel, there is a **9-frame window** where you can pick it up at the same time. The barrel keeps its momentum and will **launch in the direction it would've taken to the carrier from its new position**.

If the barrel was originally down-left from the carrier, it will launch forward-right from its new position.

The barrel typically loses its momentum very fast while carrying it, so it needs to be **placed back down quickly**.

## Setup (not perfect but works)

### Place the barrel

Stand in the very middle of the barrel pad and place the barrel straight downwards. If trying to go fast, it may be better to be **slightly too up than too down** &mdash; being more down may give it too much forward momentum and have it land in the flames.

### Line up Fox

1. Hit the switch.
2. Use Fox's angle after hitting the switch to target and line up so Fox's right foot is on the pad.
3. Try to align with the barrel &mdash; if it was placed slightly left originally, also stand slightly left of middle.
4. ESS turn to face the ladder and target.

<!-- TODO: re-host the line-up image. Was at https://www.speedrun.com/static/blob/kzwm3kjn.png -->

### Pick up timing

When the **bottom of the carrier aligns with the edge**, press A to pick up the barrel &mdash; you should still hear the sound of the carrier picking it up.

It's better to press A **too late than too early** &mdash; there's a 10th frame at the start where if you press A too early, you still hear the sound, but the barrel doesn't have momentum.

<!-- TODO: re-host the pickup timing image. Was at https://www.speedrun.com/static/blob/xzd27v9e.png -->

### Place and continue

After picking up the barrel, immediately ESS up and place the barrel &mdash; it should land at the top without falling or being destroyed by the flames.

Carry it to the other side. **Don't stand on the red pressure plates** &mdash; the extra height will explode the barrel. You can long-throw the barrel to destroy the wall.

## Risk note

There is just enough time for you to place the barrel anywhere to hit the switch as fast as possible, then re-place the barrel as it's moving &mdash; so there's no slowdown hitting the switch compared to not doing the strat. But it may be riskier to do on a good-pace run.
