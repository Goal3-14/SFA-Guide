# GQE (Ground Quake Early)

**Location:** VFP &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Camera Lock](../glitches/camera-lock.md)

**Used only in [100%](../categories/hundred-percent.md).** Skips the first Krazoa cutscene outside of VFP.

## Setup

By gaining [Cam Lock](../glitches/camera-lock.md) from:

- The **rock wall in the escalator room** in VFP, or
- The **very first room in VFP** (this way is difficult to get Tricky to comply &mdash; not recommended).

...you can:

1. Activate camera lock from the end of the moving platforms.
2. Cancel it as you leave VFP.
3. This deloads the entrance to Ground Quake, allowing you to get it without completing VFP1.
4. This also allows you to climb to MMP without planting the first moon seed.

## Timing note

This cam lock requires **more precise target timing** than other cam locks.
