# FGL (Fire Gem Launch)

**Location:** Cape Claw &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Ball Entanglement](../glitches/ball-entanglement.md)

## What it does

Allows you to get the **Cape Claw Fire Gem under the main statue** without disguise and Ice Blast &mdash; and faster than the intended route. Tricky's ball is required (15 scarabs at the shop). The Cape Claw bridge needs to be extended prior to starting.

## Setup &mdash; Ball Entanglement

This uses [Ball Entanglement](../glitches/ball-entanglement.md): hotkey Tricky's ball, then press **Y to take it out one frame before pressing A to pick up a basket**.

This needs to be performed in the correct position by **using the plant by the basket to align yourself**, then stop moving as soon as you are capable of picking up the basket (when the **A** button appears over it).

You will know the glitch worked if:

- Fox is holding the ball, **and**
- In the top right, the text next to the A button has **disappeared** (it doesn't say "throw" anymore).

Pressing Y and A on the same frame will require you to save and reload to start over.

## The launch

Once Ball Entanglement is active:

1. Cross the bridge to in front of the Krazoa Statue.
2. **Very slowly walk off the platform** at a certain spot while facing the edge completely straight.
3. Once you are off the edge a **Ball Launch** will trigger, boosting you in a direction based on the spot you picked up the basket.
4. You will land inside the opening underneath.

You will land at the very edge &mdash; to not accidentally fall off, it is recommended to either:

- **Hold R** so you can't walk off while the camera is adjusting, or
- Not move at all and just roll to go in the correct direction.

## Use case

Used in [Single File](../categories/single-file.md) with [GBS](gbs.md) to skip CCE and save ~1:30&ndash;2:00 over collecting the fire gem normally.
