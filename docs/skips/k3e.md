# K3E (Krazoa 3 Early)

**Location:** Lightfoot Village basement &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md)

Collect K3 without having completed the rest of Lightfoot Village. In ESW routes this allows delaying OFP1 to the end of the run so you can do OFP1 and OFP2 together &mdash; skipping multiple trips.

## Setup

Once you have completed [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md) and have loaded the next area, you can immediately swim under Lightfoot Village inside the basement. **Break your air swim** using the boxes on the side of the room, then walk straight to the K3 warp pad.

## The softlock and escape

After collecting K3, the basement entrance has not been opened &mdash; you are **trapped**. To escape you are required to do an [ESW](esw.md) to warp out.

You can die to trigger the ESW by:

1. Climbing up the boxes to the left as you exit the K3 warp room (the same boxes you broke your air swim on).
2. Precisely falling down to the lower ramp leading to the K3 warp.
3. The fall must be **just far enough to take fall damage**.
