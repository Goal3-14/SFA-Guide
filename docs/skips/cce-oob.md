# CCE OOB

**Location:** Lightfoot Village link map &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md)

The setup OOB used by all CCE-family skips. By using [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md), you can get out of bounds and make your way OOB to the water in the LFV link map, then use that water to **start a new air swim at a lower height**, which can be used to get to CC and K3.
