# QE Cam Lock (Queen Earthwalker Cam Lock)

**Location:** OFP &rarr; LFV &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Camera Lock](../glitches/camera-lock.md)

Skips the final Queen Earthwalker cutscene after OFP2.

## Setup

Depending on the route, you can get [Cam Lock](../glitches/camera-lock.md) from:

- The ladder at the entrance to OFP
- The final ladder leaving the CC well
- The ladder in LFV

The chosen setup is determined by route &mdash; usually whichever is on your path immediately before the cutscene trigger.
