# Damage Clip (Gate Clip)

**Location:** Ice Mountain &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Damage Clip glitch](../glitches/damage-clip.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **v1.0 only**

The Any% setup that pushes Fox out of bounds, enabling [Seam Drop](seam-drop.md) and the [Any% route](../categories/any-percent.md).

## What it does

Open the bike race cave door. Do the Gate/Damage Clip by having the **cannon hit you while holding a barrel** &mdash; you drop the barrel onto yourself, and the push clips you out of bounds.

This relies on Fox's v1.0 collision with explosive barrels &mdash; see the [Damage Clip glitch](../glitches/damage-clip.md) for why this only works on v1.0.
