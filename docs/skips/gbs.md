# GBS (Gold Bar Skip)

**Location:** Cape Claw &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md)

Skips collecting the gold bars around CC that are normally needed to rescue the Queen. The ladder will not be lowered &mdash; in categories which do Ocean Force Point 1 during CC2, you'll need [FGL](fgl.md) to skip needing the ladder to get the fire gem.

## Setup

### Past Lightfoot Village

When swimming past LFV you need to pass by the K3 basement area carefully to hit a needed loading trigger. **Do a memory leak** which will cause the next area to load in deloaded state.

1. Once past the two whirlpools, swim backwards so your **camera goes OOB but not Fox**.
2. **Do not target during this** &mdash; it will crash.
3. If the game briefly freezes you'll know the memory leak worked.
4. Pan the camera while zoomed so it's back in bounds, then continue.

### Loading Cape Claw

To load Cape Claw, swim backwards again while not targeting, making sure the camera is perpendicular to the edge of the map (just look straight forward).

### Inside Cape Claw

1. Swim to the normal entrance to hit a loading trigger to ensure the needed switch is loaded.
2. Swim where you can break your swim.
3. **Try to fall inside the head.** If you fall outside the head, walk into the head entrance &mdash; the door normally blocking you is deloaded.
4. Swim to and hit the switch to open the gas chamber gate.
5. Hit the checkpoint in the gas chamber, then save and reload.

## Note on default textures

While performing GBS the game will load areas without textures, displaying default textures instead. On v1.0 these are multi-colour; on v1.1 they're grey. See [Versions](../general-info/versions.md).
