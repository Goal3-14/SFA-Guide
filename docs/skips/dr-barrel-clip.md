# DR Barrel Clip

**Location:** Dragon Rock &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** geometry clip

You can clip a barrel through the wall into the wind tunnel &mdash; **skipping having to use the Barrel carrier to get the barrel to the upper floor**.

## Setup

1. Walk against the wall **until you're just past the edge of the shadow**.
2. Zoom and put the **left ? texture in the middle of the screen** (or very slightly left of middle).
3. Throw the barrel.

The barrel can miss the wind tunnel and fall back down, or fall into the void &mdash; **check the left side of the silo and the barrel spawner** to verify it hasn't failed.
