# Gate Despawn

**Location:** Dark Ice Mines &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** trigger dodge (rock + tree wallclip)

## What it skips

In DIM after crossing the first cog bridge and turning left, on the right of the path is a rock next to a fence. By going around the **right side** of the tree and rock, you can clip inside the fence and get past the rock from around the outside.

On the **left side** of the rock is a loading trigger which loads the large gate that the Mammoth is required to break through. Going around the rock dodges this trigger and leaves the gate **unloaded** so it can be skipped.

To clip past the gate, **roll at the far left side** of where the gate would usually be.

## Why this matters

Later, using the snow horn will still cause the Mammoth to appear even if you never feed him &mdash; and since the mammoth is initially only used to open this gate, **feeding him can be completely skipped**.

You can still kill the Sharpclaw and get Flame command even if you avoid this trigger, so you can go around the rock on the first time passing it.

## Combinations

- Pairs with [Seam Walk](seam-walk.md) as the safer DIM completion route.
- Pairs with [Cog Skip](cog-skip.md) + [Bike Skip](bike-skip.md) as the faster but riskier route.
