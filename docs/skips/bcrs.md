# BCRS (Baby Cloudrunner Skip)

**Location:** Cloudrunner Fortress &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** wind tunnel flag

Lets you skip saving the Queen and Baby Cloudrunners.

## How it works

By walking into the wind tunnel at a **very specific speed**, you can trigger the wind tunnel **without actually falling into it**. Doing this on the top floor sets a flag telling the wind tunnel to send you downwards. When properly entering the wind tunnel at the lower level, the game will still try to send you downwards &mdash; resulting in you reaching the bottom-most level **without having to switch the wind tunnel to down**.

## Skipping the gate

To skip having to open the gate, you can **clip a barrel through the wall into the wind tunnel**. By switching the wind tunnel direction while you're at the very bottom, you can get the barrel and use it to reach the boss.

## Prep

The grating above the wind tunnel needs to **already be broken**, so we stand on it to break it then save-quit back to outside the gate and can do the trick.
