# CCE (Cape Claw Early)

**Location:** Cape Claw &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md) &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [CCE OOB](cce-oob.md)

Lets you enter Cape Claw without needing the larger wallet to get 60 scarabs, **skipping K2** and fully returning the first spellstone. Now typically only used to enter **CC2 and CC3**; CC1 has its own version ([Gold Bar Skip](gbs.md)).

## Setup

1. Once you load the LFV link map while OOB, swim underneath the map in the same path as you would when not OOB.
2. Once you get far enough along close to the well ladder, the well will load &mdash; swim to the well exit to CC.
3. **Cancel your swim on the arch of the well exit.**
4. To get the area to load properly, go through the exit to hit a trigger before the corner, then go back.
5. Roll down the ladder and hit a trigger just before the other ladder that goes up on that level.
6. Continue to CC as normal.

If you want to use the cannon in Cape Claw, hug the left wall to make sure you hit a loading trigger for the cannon.
