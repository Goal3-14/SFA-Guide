# Seam Walk

**Location:** Dark Ice Mines &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Considered glitchless &mdash; allowed in all categories.**

## What it does

After using the switch at the top of the area to lower the bridge, you can **drop off the bridge onto the conveyor railing** and walk along the railing to **get to the boss fight early**.

## Setup

The railing floor is extremely thin. When dropping down to the warp you need to **start holding up immediately as you start falling** in order to fall forward enough.

## Video

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tKWkQBz6mAo>

## When to use this vs Bike Skip

[Bike Skip](bike-skip.md) is significantly faster but very difficult and high-risk. Seam Walk is the safer, easier-to-learn DIM completion &mdash; about 5:30 slower.
