# Early Cheat Token

**Location:** Lightfoot Village &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Used in:** [100%](../categories/hundred-percent.md)

A falling-clip past the invisible wall at the LFV cheat token ledge.

## Position

Walking against the invisible wall, slowly move right until **there's a small gap between the edge of the ledge and Fox's right foot**. Be careful not to go too far right as you can fall.

## Angle

Once in position:

1. **Zoom and turn until the tree is in the middle of the screen.**
2. When you unzoom and are targeting, the spellstone should be in the reference position &mdash; **as close to it as possible**.

<!-- TODO: re-host the spellstone-position image. Was at https://www.speedrun.com/static/blob/5ngorg4e.png -->

## The fall

Once you have the position and angle, **hold target**.

1. **Lightly tap left** so you aren't at the very edge.
2. **Tap right** to fall off the edge.

You **don't want to fall as close to the wall as possible** &mdash; you need a bit of momentum to go far enough, but don't want to keep that momentum too much. So we tap left before each fall attempt to tap right harder for more momentum without going too far right.

Once you go off the right, **keep holding target**.

### Outcomes

- **No fall:** you'll land back where you started &mdash; try again, tap left then right.
- **Fox slides down the wall slightly before popping back up more forward past the invisible wall:** **do not move** &mdash; just hold target and roll, and you should safely get to the other side.
