# DIME (Dark Ice Mines Early)

**Location:** Sewer drop &rarr; DIM &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Glitch used:** [Air Swim](../glitches/air-swim.md)

Unlocks DIM on the first visit to SHW without needing to get there normally.

## Loading gotcha

When first going OOB with the swim, you can't go too far away from the map otherwise you will **miss a loading trigger** and the sewer won't load &mdash; this is probably the most common mistake out of any new player ever.
