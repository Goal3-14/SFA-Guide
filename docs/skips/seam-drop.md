# Seam Drop

**Location:** Ice Mountain &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; **Prereq:** [Damage Clip](damage-clip.md)

## What it does

After getting OOB via [Damage Clip](damage-clip.md), Seam Drop requires **extremely precise position and angle** to land on a **specific pixel** out of bounds, getting above the cave and dropping into it to trigger [Bike Flag Glitch](../glitches/bike-flag-glitch.md).

## Difficulty

One of the three most precise tricks in [Any%](../categories/any-percent.md). The 2-File route does Seam Drop early (after first reaching IM); the Normal route does it after Damage Clip. The TK5E route adds further difficulty by requiring Seam Drop during a strict memory-manip time window.
