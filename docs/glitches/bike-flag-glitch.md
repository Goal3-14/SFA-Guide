# Bike Flag Glitch

<!-- TODO: detailed writeup pending. Source guide had this as a placeholder. -->

In Ice Mountain, after triggering the bike race cave door and using [Damage Clip](../skips/damage-clip.md) and [Seam Drop](../skips/seam-drop.md), this glitch allows you to **drive the bike around freely**, outside the constraints of the race.

This is a core building block of the [Any%](../categories/any-percent.md) route &mdash; the bike serves as the "mount" for [Void Travel](void-travel.md) to the K5 shrine.

## Used by

- [K5E](../skips/k5e.md)
- [Any% route](../categories/any-percent.md)
