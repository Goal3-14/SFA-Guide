# Mammoth Clip

The DIM Mammoth can abuse certain wall collisions to **partially clip into walls**.

## Setup

Follow the wall to the right of the ice wall, then at the next wall hold between **up and up-right** on your controller to turn slightly &mdash; you'll partially clip into the wall.

## Two outcomes

From the partial-clip state you can either:

- **Fully clip through and drop down** &rarr; sets up [Bike Skip](../skips/bike-skip.md).
- **Turn right immediately** to climb the wall up to the cannon &rarr; sets up [Cog Skip](../skips/cog-skip.md).
