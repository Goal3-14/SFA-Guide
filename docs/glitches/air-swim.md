# Air Swim

In Thorntail Hollow, the water on top of the waterfall extends slightly over the edge. By slowly going off the edge of the waterfall at the correct spot, you can land in this water and begin swimming while being off the waterfall. Swimming only gets cancelled when Fox steps on the ground, meaning you can freely **swim in the air** until you next hit the ground.

This allows getting out of bounds in multiple places.

## Swim height

Depending on where you go off the waterfall, you can swim at slightly different heights, which affects certain downstream tricks.

While swimming, Fox bobs up and down by 1 unit in each direction, causing his height to vary slightly &mdash; important at certain times. You swim at the height of the water you originally entered, but can change height by interacting with other water at a height close to your current height.

## Used by

Air Swim is a **prerequisite for all variants** of both [Cape Claw Early (CCE)](../skips/cce.md) and [Dark Ice Mines Early (DIME)](../skips/dime.md), which form the core tricks of the game's glitched categories.

- For **CCE variants**, any swim height can be used.
- For **DIME variants**, a more precise air swim setup is needed to guarantee getting the maximum height &mdash; it's required for the trick.
