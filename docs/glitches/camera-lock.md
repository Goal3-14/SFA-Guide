# Camera Lock

## What it is

When activating a certain camera mode (such as by feeding Tricky or talking to an NPC), **on the same frame** as your camera position being set (such as by starting to crawl or climb), Fox will fail to do the crawl or climb &mdash; but the camera will still get fixed close up just behind Fox and will stay in that position until forcibly changed (e.g. by targeting).

## Why it matters

In certain locations &mdash; typically near map boundaries &mdash; triggering the camera mode again will cause the camera to get stuck. Many triggers in the game are **activated when the camera goes through them, not when Fox does**. By freezing the camera we can safely go through the triggers without the camera activating them, allowing certain areas to deload or certain cutscenes to skip.

## Used by

- [Beacon Skip](../skips/beacon-skip.md) &mdash; skip the MMP gate key cutscene.
- [QE Cam Lock](../skips/qe-cam-lock.md) &mdash; skip the final Queen Earthwalker cutscene.
- [GQE](../skips/gqe.md) &mdash; 100% only; skip the first Krazoa cutscene outside VFP.
- [OFP Cam Lock](../skips/ofp-cam-lock.md) &mdash; leave OFP without opening the fire gem door.
