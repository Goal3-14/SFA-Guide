# Waterfall Jump

Lets you get **out of bounds at the beginning of Walled City**.

## Setup

By jumping off at an angle at the edge of the waterfall, you can land on a raised ground at the waterfall edge. With precise position and angle, you can trigger a jump and land out of bounds.
